# Techuilla Agency

> **Creative Technology & Digital Marketing Studio**  
> Bringing ambitious ideas to life with 3D animation, AI creatives, and world-class digital experiences.

---

## ⚡ Highlights

- **3D Animation & AI Creatives Flagship Showcase**: Interactive scroll-driven character convergence with ultra-crisp 4K character assets and hardware-accelerated transforms.
- **Elevated Page Pull-Up Animation**: Smooth drawer transition starting at 60% scrub that pulls the statement page into view while converging background elements continue their inward momentum.
- **Dynamic Horizontal Photo Gallery**: 9-image staggered layout with seamless directional scroll conversion (vertical-to-horizontal) and color-shifting orange backdrop.
- **Three Major Problems Showcase**: Scroll-paced narrative highlighting digital growth leaks and strategic solutions.
- **Floating Minimalist Navigation**: Ultra-clean brand header with floating enquiry triggers and full-screen menu drawer transition.
- **Responsive & Performance Optimized**: Pure CSS and zero-lag hardware-accelerated animations running at 60 FPS.
